FAQ

1. What is Xnail Cloud?

Xnail Cloud is a shop-in-shop model where a dedicated Xnail nail business is established inside an existing salon or suitable beauty location.

2. How much investment is required?

The Xnail Cloud model requires ₹3.10 lakh + GST, while the Exclusive Outlet model is positioned at ₹10 lakh as per your current franchise offer.

3. What is the revenue-sharing model?

For Xnail Cloud, the current offer is 5% MG or 20% of sales, whichever is higher. For the Exclusive Outlet, it is 3% MG or 30% of net sales, whichever is higher.

4. Do I need to be a nail artist?

No. The business model is designed for entrepreneurs and investors, with training and operational support.

5. Does Xnail provide training?

Yes. Xnail's brand model includes professionally trained/certified artists and education support. (XNail)

6. Can an existing salon take Xnail Cloud?

Yes. This is one of the primary applications of the shop-in-shop model.

7. What support does Xnail provide?

Setup, training, product support, marketing and operational guidance are part of the franchise proposition. (XNail)
