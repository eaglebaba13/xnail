# Xnail Bar Franchise Landing Page

## Build
- Replace the blank page with a premium, mobile-first franchise landing page using black, champagne gold, and Xnail pink.
- Use the supplied Xnail logo prominently and derive a butterfly favicon from it.
- Create an immersive opening section with luxury nail imagery, both investment options, direct call actions, and a persistent mobile/desktop conversion bar.
- Structure the full supplied content into scannable sections: proof, story, market opportunity, both models, comparison, benefits, ecosystem, milestones, support, revenue, partner fit, cities, FAQ, and final call-to-action.
- Add a concise lead form with Name, Mobile, City, and Model, including clear validation and click-to-call follow-up.

## Visual direction
- Dark luxury editorial styling with sharp, restrained cards and champagne/pink accents.
- Large expressive typography, premium nail photography, subtle motion, and strong investment-price hierarchy.
- Fully responsive with no overlap and readable content across phone and desktop screens.

## Technical details
- Define all visual values as semantic design tokens in the global design system.
- Add unique page metadata and social tags for the franchise page.
- Keep the form frontend-only and provide a clear submitted state; no lead storage or messaging integration is included.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes.
